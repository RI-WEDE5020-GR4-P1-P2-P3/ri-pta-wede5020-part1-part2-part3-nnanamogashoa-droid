Brief History

SWVY was founded in 2025 by Zack and friends. From the beginning the brand decided that customers should be able to shop in two ways: they can order online 3or they can order online and then collect the items from the physical store at a time that suits them and the time slots of when the store is open. This approach was chosen because many South African shoppers like the flexibility of choosing the method that fits their day-to-day life.
The FreakWrld undergarments range was added so that customers could buy both outer clothing and undergarments from the same brand, because undergarments are personal items.
In its first year SWVY built a basic online store and opened a small number of physical stores. The online stores work, but they do not yet show live stock levels for each store and it has not yet chosen the best delivery method automatically.

Mission & Vision

To give customers a modern and easy shopping experience that combines good quality contemporary clothing with clear information about stock and reliable delivery or collection options. To become a trusted clothing retailer in South Africa that is known for treating customers well, showing honest stock information, and making both home delivery and store collection simple and dependable.
